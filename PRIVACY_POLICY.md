# Privacy Policy for ReqCraft - HTTP Request & Mock Tool

**Effective Date:** October 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

ReqCraft ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data for the **ReqCraft - HTTP Request & Mock Tool** Chrome Extension.

## 2. Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal data, user credentials, or browsing history.**

- **Local Storage:** Any configurations, mock endpoints, headers, or request data created within the extension are stored locally on your device via Chrome's local storage APIs (`chrome.storage`). This data never leaves your browser and is not transmitted to any external servers or third parties.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics, tracking scripts, or telemetry to monitor your usage.

## 3. Permissions

ReqCraft requires specific browser permissions solely to fulfill its core features (making HTTP requests and mocking API responses as directed by the user). These permissions are strictly used client-side and are not utilized to gather personal information.

## 4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
